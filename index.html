Crumbly: Kids ABC & 123

Toddler & Preschool Learning

Crumbly is a pretend-play bakery for children aged 2 to 7. Animal customers come in, your child mixes, bakes, decorates and serves a treat, and gets a sticker. When you're ready, switch on letters or numbers and the orders quietly become little lessons.

Download on the App Store

For iPhone and iPad. English for now.

  • You

    Parents choose the learning

    Turn on ABC, 123, both, or neither for pure pretend play. Levels move up on their own as your child learns, or you can pick one. More learning layers are coming.

  • ABC

    Letters

    Customers ask for letter cookies: letter names, then letter sounds, first sounds ("B for banana"), and simple three-letter words. Clear, easy-to-read letters.

  • 123

    Numbers

    Count toppings aloud onto a treat, from 1–5 up to 10, match numerals to amounts, and learn "one more".

  • ☺

    Calm and simple

    Big buttons, no reading needed, no timers, no scores, and no "wrong" buzzers, just gentle hints. Made for iPhone and iPad, and it works offline.
